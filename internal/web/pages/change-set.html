Retry webhook delivery with backoff

acceptedrunning in 01M6PF31 3 leaves · 1 with evidence1 open question

Intent

Why this exists

Webhook deliveries that fail today are dropped after one attempt. Retry them with exponential backoff so a receiver that is briefly down still gets every event, without retrying so hard that we become the reason it stays down.
