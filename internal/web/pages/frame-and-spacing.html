The rules

Header56px on a phone, 52px on desktop; 16px side padding; sticky
Header to page headspace-6 (32) on a phone, space-7 (48) on desktop
Kicker to page titlespace-3 (12)
Page title to its linespace-3 (12)
Page head to contentspace-6 (32)
Panel kicker to panel title4, never more
Panel title to contentspace-4 (16)
Between panelsspace-5 (24); between page sections space-6 (32)
Panel paddingspace-4 (16) on a phone, space-5 (24) on desktop
List rows10 above and below, 1px line between; 44px minimum when the row is tappable
Spacing methodGaps on the parent (flex or grid gap), never margins on children; headings carry margin 0
