## statement

The plan workflow is an agent step driven by a plan guide topic, planning a milestone or a single change set straight into the model: facts are looked up, never asked; one topic at a time with a recommendation; picks through AskUserQuestion so they are recorded with the right provenance; parked items as questions; the conversation as a Source with Decisions quoting the owner's typed words; a fresh plan review (missing acceptance, missing provenance, unreal depends-on, scope, contradictions) with a critic and at most 3 rounds; acceptance as the gate.
