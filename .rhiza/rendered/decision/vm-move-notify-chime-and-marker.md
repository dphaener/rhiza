# vm-move-notify-chime-and-marker

decision · `01M6P8WMSF00T3GAPX4B4M3WS7`

## statement

A question in an agent tab on the VM notifies the owner on the Mac by Rex's chime and the blocked marker on the tab and in the session picker; that is enough for this milestone. Rex's program-status protocol (rev 0.4) leaves presentation to the terminal and promises no macOS banner: in the owner's live test on 2026-11-05 every blocked report from a VM tab chimed on the Mac when the tab was not focused (tests C to G) and one showed a banner (test B). Reliable banners are a Rex upstream request (docs/rex-upstream/notification-banner.md)

## wording

Chime + tab marker is enough: accept what Rex does today, the chime plus the blocked marker on the tab and in the session picker, as the notification; ask Rex upstream for reliable banners separately, the agent's option, picked by the owner
