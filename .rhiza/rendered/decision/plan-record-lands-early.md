# plan-record-lands-early

decision · `01M5XG41ZYPSR6CTS1DP0BMQ6K`

## ruling

the planning record lands as soon as the interview is written, before review, as milestones 2 and 3 did

## statement

A milestone plan's planning record lands as soon as the interview is written, before review: the plan run starts on a draft opened as a record change set; the planner writes the milestone intent, its done points, the Decisions and the Source into it and lands it with rhiza land, then writes the leaves of the change sets planned now, which refer to the record's landed nodes; the change sets it opened stay drafts for the gate, and the reviser fixes nodes in the landed record with --record writes. Decisions captured from the interview after the planner exits are written as a record write.

## wording

the orchestrator's ruling as arbiter for the owner, in the plan-workflow planning session
