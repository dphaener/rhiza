## statement

The engine calls an arbiter only at decision points (a session's question, a plan contradiction, a leaf failing after 3 tries, work outside the intent). In normal mode these go to the owner; in autonomous mode a fresh arbiter session told only the question's ID decides from slice and why and records (assumed, would have escalated) with its reasoning. One arbiter serves every workflow.
