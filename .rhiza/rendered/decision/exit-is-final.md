# exit-is-final

decision · `01M5WC0Y8JK6CXNDDTVPWR275C`

## quote

status precedence applies among a live session's reports. Once the process has exited, the exit is the status, and no earlier hook or Rex report outranks it. A hook or Rex report that arrives after the exit doesn't revive the session.
