## statement

plan auto: the daemon runs a requested plan autonomously; plan owner: it opens the plan run in a tab for the owner to drive. accept auto: change sets an owner-requested plan proposes are accepted at its gate when its review passes; accept owner: they stay proposed and the inbox lists each as waiting for acceptance. ship auto: the daemon starts ship runs for accepted change sets; ship owner: only the owner starts them (roadmap app s). signoff auto: ship runs are autonomous and the arbiter signs off; signoff owner: ship runs stop at sign-off for the owner (inbox approve). paused: the daemon starts and resumes nothing but keeps polling, reporting and notifying
