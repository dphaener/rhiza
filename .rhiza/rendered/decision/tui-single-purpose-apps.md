# tui-single-purpose-apps

## quote

multiple small apps. single purpose. i don't want the tui to dominate the terminal. think in and out. the proposed actions seem good.

## quote_where

tui-small-apps-interview, typed answer 2, on the shape of the apps

## statement

The TUI is multiple small single-purpose apps that never dominate the terminal: each opens, does its one job and closes (in and out). This supersedes tui-many-mini-apps: fewer apps, each with a single purpose, instead of many mini apps.
